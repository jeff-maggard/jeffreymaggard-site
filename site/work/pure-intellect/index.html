Pure Intellect Tutor, Analyzer & AI Ranking App

AI that coaches students to think, and helps professors grade fairly.

The challenge
Students can ask AI to do the work for them. I wanted AI that makes them think harder instead.
Audience
First-year university students and their professors.

02Pure Intellect Tutor

The tutor is a Socratic coach. It asks one question at a time and never writes the answer.

  • Replies stay under 60 words.
  • Students get at most three hints per step.
  • A code-level guard turns any finished-work reply into a question.

03Pure Intellect Analyzer

The Analyzer is for professors only. It suggests a rubric level for each criterion and quotes the student’s own words as evidence. The professor accepts or changes every level.

  • Feedback the professor chooses: a strength, a “try this” and one question back.
  • An AI-use check against the course policy. It never guesses whether text was written by AI.
  • One optional revision, where the higher score counts.
  • A gradebook with Excel and CSV export.

04AI Ranking App

The project began as the AI Use Rating App: a voluntary “show your work” portal that coaches how people think while they use AI. It uses no AI detection and no keystroke tracking.

05Design principles

  • Accessibility first: WCAG 2.2 AA, designed for Deaf and hard-of-hearing students.
  • No timers and no leaderboards.
  • Student data stays on the student’s device.

06Quality

48 automated tests, plus a scripted click-through on desktop and phone.
