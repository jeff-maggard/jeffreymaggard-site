01Course map

Nine units and 108 lessons, built on public-domain nonfiction by Lincoln, Douglass, Wollstonecraft, Wells, Du Bois and others.

Each lesson pairs a short video, a guided activity and a three-question quiz.

02Sample lesson package

“Reading the Rhetorical Situation” (Unit 1, Lesson 1):

  • A video script with visual cues.
  • Printable guided notes using Elizabeth I’s Tilbury speech.
  • A five-question quiz with feedback on every answer.
  • An original four-level rubric.

03Video scripts

Three teleprompter scripts: “What Counts as Evidence?”, “A Thesis That Needs Defending” and “Linking Evidence to Your Claim.”

Each has timecodes, on-screen cues, a 20-second practice pause and a scaffolded quiz (identify, apply, analyze).
