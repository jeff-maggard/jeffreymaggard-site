The seminar

I co-created the Peace Studies Seminar on Cambodia with Dr. Elizabeth Lokon. The Cambodian Ambassador’s Counsellor, embassy staff from Japan, 11 MIC faculty, plus NGO staff gave presentations.

It served more than 200 students and community members.

The trip

After the seminar, I led a student trip to Phnom Penh and Siem Reap (February 2004) to meet with an NGO (Vulnerable Children’s Assistance Organization).

Cambodia

Photos from the student trip. Children’s faces are blurred for privacy.

The Peace Studies Club

As the Peace Studies Club faculty advisor, I:

  • Coordinated fundraising with MIC’s Drama, Music, Dance and Peace Studies Clubs to support a Khmer NGO, the Vulnerable Children’s Assistance Organization (2003–2004).
  • Prepared students to present our paper, “Young Japanese people’s views on civic (dis)engagement,” at the 2nd Peace as a Global Language Conference in Tokyo (2003).
  • Co-presented our research results from “Developing social responsibility and IC skills” at the 3rd Peace as a Global Language Conference in Kyoto (2004).
