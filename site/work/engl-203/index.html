Course design · AI

ENGL 203: AI-Enhanced Academic Research and Writing

A writing course where AI use is visible, logged and defended.

The challenge
Students now research and write with AI. The course makes that use transparent.
Audience
University students, with differentiation for B1 and C1 learners.
My role
Course designer (proposal under review at Qatar University).
Tools
  • Syllabus
  • Scope and sequence
  • Rubrics
  • AI use policy

On this page

  1. Structure
  2. Assessment

01Structure

A 15-week syllabus with scope and sequence in three phases:

  1. AI literacy and research.
  2. Writing as process.
  3. Presentation and reflection.

02Assessment

Students keep an AI Research Log. They defend their choices in an oral defense.

The course includes rubrics, an AI use policy and differentiation for B1 and C1 learners.
