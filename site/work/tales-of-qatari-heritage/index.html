Tales of Qatari Heritage

I am lead editor of a bilingual book of Qatari folktales, leading six co-editors and more than 150 students, faculty and staff.

My role
Lead editor, working with six co-editors. Chair of the Cultural Awareness Team (2016–2023).
Competencies

Core

  • Leading more than 150 students, faculty and staff
  • Training students to collect stories from elders
  • Bilingual editing with six co-editors
  • Faculty mentoring and stakeholder engagement
  • Active and applied learning

The purpose

To preserve and share Qatar’s folktales, fables and legends, and to give students a way to serve their community.

The book

Tales of Qatari Heritage is an unpublished bilingual (Arabic/English) book of Qatari folktales collected by Qatar University students. Most stories show compassion and forgiveness.

My role

I am lead editor, working with six co-editors. As Chair of the Cultural Awareness Team (2016–2023), I led more than 150 students, faculty and staff on the project.
