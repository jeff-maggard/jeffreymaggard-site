01The book

Tales of Forgiveness is an unpublished bilingual (Arabic/English) book of Qatari folktales collected by Qatar University students. The stories show compassion and forgiveness.

02My role

I am lead editor, working with six co-editors. As Chair of the Cultural Awareness Team (2016–2023), I led more than 150 students, faculty and staff on the project.
