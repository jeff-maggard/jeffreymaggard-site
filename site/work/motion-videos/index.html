The purpose

Products and courses need short videos that explain them fast.

What I make

  • Product launch videos.
  • Course website walkthroughs, captioned to work with no sound.
  • Portfolio and introduction videos.
  • Formats for every channel: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 for LinkedIn.

How I build them

Every frame is coded in React with Remotion. Captions and graphics are timed to the measured voice track. The music bed is an original piano score. I write every script.
