The purpose

To preserve Emirati oral traditions and give students a public audience for their writing.

The project

I co-founded the Al Kharareef storytelling club at Zayed University in 2011 and was its student advisor. The club ran storytelling events, including World Storytelling Day and visits to schools. That work caught the attention of the Abu Dhabi Music & Arts Foundation, which invited us to take part in the Abu Dhabi Festival, sponsored by Mubadala.

In 2013, I invited the world-famous master storyteller Abdul Aziz Al Musallam, head of the Sharjah Institute for Heritage, to Zayed University. He helped participating faculty & students craft their stories the way they were meant to be told.

  1. A writing unit

    I added a fiction and folktale unit to our course textbook: how to collect, write and tell stories. The department approved it.

  2. 500+ writers

    More than 500 students wrote stories as part of the course.

  3. A contest

    I organized the faculty and got a writing contest approved. Volunteers entered their stories.

  4. Editing

    I was lead editor of the winning stories. Brione LaThrop, the managing editor, handled publishing and permissions.

On the Corniche

The Story Mile: A Journey Through Emirati Oral Traditions opened on the Abu Dhabi Corniche as part of the 10th Abu Dhabi Festival on 10 March 2013. It was planned to close on 29 March, but it was so popular that it stayed up for six months. Admission was free.

Forty stories were exhibited on lightboxes that ran for about a mile along the Corniche boardwalk, which gave the project its name. Each lightbox showed two stories in English, one on each side. Some were passed down through generations. Others were new stories that students adapted or combined.

World Storytelling Day

The Al Kharareef Club also took part in a number of events, including the World Storytelling Day event in Abu Dhabi on 20 March 2013. Club members told stories to visiting schoolchildren at Abu Dhabi Heritage Village. Children’s faces are blurred for their privacy.

School visits

The Kharareef Club also did a number of school visits to hold storytelling events. One was at Mushrif School on 12 March 2013.

The outcome

  • A six-month public exhibition on the Abu Dhabi Corniche.
  • A permanent exhibition at Khalifa Park, still there today.
  • Brione LaThrop took the stories to Zayed University Press. We edited them further, and The Story Mile was published in 2014 (ISBN 978-9948-20-117-5).
