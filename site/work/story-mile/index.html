01The project

I founded the Al Kharareef storytelling club at Zayed University and was its faculty supervisor. Brione LaThrop joined me later.

I invited the master storyteller Abdul Aziz Al Musallam, head of the Ministry of Culture in Sharjah, to Zayed University. He helped students craft their stories the way they were meant to be told.

I co-created the Story Mile Project with Mubadala, the Sharjah Institute for Heritage and the Abu Dhabi Music & Arts Festival.

02On the Corniche

The Story Mile: A Journey Through Emirati Oral Traditions opened on the Abu Dhabi Corniche as part of the 10th Abu Dhabi Festival, 10–29 March 2013. Admission was free.

Lightboxes along the seafront path showed 40 stories by Zayed University students and the Al Kharareef Club. Some were passed down through generations. Others were new stories that students adapted or combined.

In the press: “Abu Dhabi’s storytelling tradition kept alive along the Corniche”, The National, 13 March 2013.

03The outcome

  • 300 student authors took part.
  • A public exhibition on the Abu Dhabi Corniche.
  • A permanent installation at Khalifa Park.
  • The book The Story Mile (ISBN 978-9948-20-117-5), which I edited.
